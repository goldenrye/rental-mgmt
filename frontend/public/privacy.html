Rental Management

Privacy & Terms

Effective October 25, 2026

Rental Management (rental-mgmt.us) is a free tool, run by an individual, that helps landlords track properties, leases, tenants, bills and payments. By creating an account you agree to this page. Questions or requests: jianwpi@gmail.com.

Privacy

What we store

  • Your account: name, display name, email address and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Rental records you enter: properties, units, leases, rent and property bills, payments, and the tenant names, emails, phone numbers and notes you add.
  • Security records: sign-in sessions, last sign-in and activity times, and IP addresses used to limit password guessing and password-reset requests.
  • Optional features, only if you turn them on: a Google Calendar connection token, browser push-notification subscriptions, and AI-agent (MCP) API keys, which are stored only as hashes.

How we use it

Only to run the service for you: show your records, generate bills, send password-reset emails and the reminders you enable, and keep accounts secure. We do not sell or rent your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies. Your sign-in token is kept in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie.

The administrator can see each account's name, email, status and sign-in activity in order to manage invitations and accounts, and has technical access to the server. We look at your rental records only when you ask for help or to fix a problem.

Who else handles it

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the server and database in the United States.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers password-reset emails, so it sees your email address and the reset message.
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it, receives the rent-bill events you choose to export.
  • Your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla), only if you enable reminders, delivers notifications that contain counts and dates, not tenant details.

How it is protected

All traffic uses HTTPS. In Edit Profile you can turn on encryption, which encrypts your rental records (AES-256-GCM) before they are written to the database; Google Calendar tokens are always encrypted. Database backups are kept for 14 days. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use elsewhere.

Tenant information

You are responsible for having the right to record your tenants' information and for following the privacy and landlord-tenant laws that apply to you.

Deleting your data

Email jianwpi@gmail.com from your account's address to ask for a copy of your data or for your account and records to be deleted. We will do it within 30 days; deleted data disappears from backups as they expire, within 14 more days.
